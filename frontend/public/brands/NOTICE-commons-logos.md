# Logos from Wikimedia Commons

Some logos here come from Wikimedia Commons file pages whose licence section says the artwork is in the public domain
(PD-textlogo or PD-shape: too simple to be copyrightable; PD-USGov: made by a US government employee) or CC0. The page,
licence text and date retrieved for each are in `frontend/brands/sources.json`; the cleaned file (shapes and colours only)
is served from here, and the app never contacts Wikimedia.

A logo can be public domain as artwork and still be a **registered trademark**. Commons tags every file here "Trademarked".
These marks stay the property of their owners and are shown only to identify the bank, airline, hotel, merchant or program
the member holds or uses. No endorsement is implied. Remove a file here (and its entry in `sources.json`) at the owner's request.
